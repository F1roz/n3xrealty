Our LinkedIn Growth System — Step by Step

Here's exactly how we build your LinkedIn presence into a lead generation asset over 90 days:

Week 1–2

Profile Transformation

We completely rebuild your LinkedIn profile. Professional headline optimized for search, keyword-rich about section, custom banner, featured section with lead magnets, and skills endorsements — turning your profile from a résumé into a landing page that converts visitors into connections who trust you.

Week 2–4

Connection Campaign

Strategic outreach to 50–100 targeted connections per week — local business owners, corporate HR managers, wealth managers, and investors in your metro area. Not random connections: curated relationships with people who either become clients or send you clients.

Month 2

Thought Leadership Content

We write and publish 3–4 posts per week using your voice: market analysis, deal breakdowns, investment insights, neighborhood spotlights, and real transaction stories. This content positions you as the market expert — not just a license holder — and triggers the LinkedIn algorithm to push your content to non-followers in your industry.

Month 2–3

Long-Form Articles & Newsletters

Monthly LinkedIn articles (500–1,200 words) on topics your ideal clients search: "Best neighborhoods for investors in [City]," "Is now the right time to buy?" and "What executives relocating to [City] need to know." These articles rank on LinkedIn search AND Google — creating permanent inbound traffic.

Month 3+

Inbound Lead Conversion

By month three, warm connection requests and inbound DMs begin arriving. We install a response sequence: a genuine value-first message followed by a market report or home valuation offer — converting cold connections into booked calls without aggressive sales tactics.
